# Traffic-EDA
🔹 Overall Analysis
The dataset contains traffic vehicle counts collected from different junctions over time. The analysis helps understand traffic flow, peak hours, busy junctions, and traffic trends.
🚦 Major Findings
1. Rush Hour Traffic
Traffic is highest during:
Morning: 8 AM – 10 AM
Evening: 5 PM – 7 PM
➡ This indicates office/school commuting hours.
2. Weekday vs Weekend
Weekdays have significantly more traffic.
Weekends show reduced traffic flow.
➡ Most traffic comes from regular daily commuters.
3. Junction Performance
Junctions 1, 2, and 3 are the busiest.
Junction 4 has very low or missing traffic records.
➡ Junction 4 may have low activity or incomplete data collection.
4. Traffic Trend Over Time
Traffic volume gradually increases over months/years.
➡ Indicates urban growth and increasing vehicle usage.
5. Night-Time Traffic
Traffic is lowest during late-night hours.
➡ Best time for:
Road maintenance
Construction work
Traffic system updates
